## Fundprint

**Who owns America's autism therapy clinics?** Fundprint traces private-equity
ownership of US applied behavior analysis (ABA) clinics, site by site, from public
sources only. Every clinic is named, located, and tied to a source document that was
fetched and content-hashed.

**Private equity owns at least 1,621 autism therapy clinics across 42 states**, about
2.8 times the deal-based count in the peer-reviewed literature. A hand check of a random
sample found 98.5% of PE clinics correct on site, address, brand and owner.
